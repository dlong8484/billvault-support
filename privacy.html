BillVault Privacy Policy

Effective 4 September 2026

The short version

BillVault collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud. The assistant runs on the device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Information the app collects

None. BillVault has no sign-up, no login and no user accounts. It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, advertising identifier, device identifiers or usage analytics, and it has no servers of its own.

Information you enter

Bills, subscriptions, budgets, savings goals, income, account balances, notes and payment history are stored in a database on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, BillVault also stores a copy in your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so your other devices stay in step. That copy is encrypted by Apple, is readable only by you, and is governed by Apple's iCloud terms. You can turn it off for BillVault in Settings, and deleting the app removes the local copy.

Card details

If you use the card vault, card numbers and security codes are stored in the device Keychain, protected by the device passcode, and never leave the device. BillVault never transmits them and never uses them to pay anything.

Apple Intelligence

Bill scanning, statement import, the daily briefing, the Ask tab and Reason it through use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Photos, statements, questions and figures are processed on the device. They are not sent to Apple's servers, to the developer or to any AI provider. Every amount shown is calculated by the app itself.

Photos, camera and files

When you scan a bill, the photo is read on the device to extract the amount and due date, then discarded. Statements you open or share into BillVault are read on the device and the file is deleted after import. The app asks for camera or photo access only when you use those features.

Face ID, Touch ID and notifications

The optional app lock uses Apple's system authentication. BillVault never receives face or fingerprint data and stores no passcode. Reminders are scheduled on the device and need no internet connection.

Purchases

BillVault Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and handles refunds; the app only learns whether Pro is active. The developer never sees your payment details.

Payment links

A bill can hold a link to a biller's own website. Opening it takes you to that site in Safari, where that company's privacy policy applies.

Children

BillVault is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.
